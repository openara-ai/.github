# openara.ai

# AI Systems

**Building AI systems through practical engineering.**

openara.ai is an independent engineering, consulting, and research practice focused on building AI systems. Our current work spans multimodal AI, model training and evaluation, local LLMs, efficient inference, and intelligent applications.

We use **agentic engineering** to shorten the **idea-to-production** cycle while maintaining **production-quality engineering**. AI agents accelerate exploration, implementation, testing, documentation, and iteration. Human engineers remain responsible for architecture, engineering judgment, quality, and final decisions.

> 📖 **The engineering stories live at [The Agentic Notes](https://theagenticnotes.substack.com)** — engineering notes on building AI systems through agentic engineering.

---

# Current Technical Focus

Our work currently explores AI systems across several complementary areas.

- **Multimodal AI** — perception across images, video, text, and metadata
- **Model Training** — improving AI capabilities through learning
- **Model Evaluation** — measuring quality, ranking, and retrieval performance
- **Local LLM Systems** — reasoning and tool use with local models
- **AI Inference** — efficient deployment across CPUs, GPUs, and NPUs
- **Edge & Embedded AI** — AI systems that run close to the data

These areas represent durable engineering capabilities that continue to evolve as AI models and hardware improve.

# How We Build

Agentic engineering is our engineering methodology.

Rather than treating AI agents as coding assistants, we treat them as members of an engineering team. Human engineers provide architecture, technical direction, engineering standards, and quality gates, while AI agents accelerate implementation, testing, documentation, and iteration.

The objective is simple:

> **Shorten the idea-to-production cycle while maintaining production-quality engineering.**

---

# Engineering Principles

- Local-first where practical
- Privacy-first by design
- Production-quality engineering
- Open source where appropriate
- Evidence over claims

We prefer demonstrating engineering through working software, documentation, benchmarks, and public repositories rather than broad claims.
